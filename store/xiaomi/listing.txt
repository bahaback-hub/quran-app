5) FULL DESCRIPTION — ENGLISH  (paste as-is)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Al-Mushaf As-Sulaimani is a complete offline Quran app with no ads and no tracking.

• Uthmani Mushaf: all 604 pages in the official Mushaf font, with a comfortable dark mode.
• Recitation: 30+ reciters, with word-by-word highlighting while you listen.
• Tafsir: Al-Muyassar included, plus additional tafsir sources online.
• Tajweed: colour-coded marks that show the tajweed rules inside every ayah.
• Prayer times & Qibla: accurate times for your city, with a Qibla compass.
• Adhkar: morning/evening and post-prayer adhkar, with optional daily reminders.
• Hifz room: a daily memorisation plan with ayah repeat and a focus mode.
• Presentation: a calm full-screen ayah display for sharing in a gathering.
• Works offline: download the mushaf and recitations once, then use them anywhere.
• Search: fast full-text search across the whole Quran.
• 8 languages: Arabic, English, Turkish, French, German, Russian, Malay, Indonesian.

Privacy first: the app collects no personal data, contains no ads and no trackers.
Everything you save (favourites, plans, reading statistics) stays on your device.
Tafsir and content come from the open sources credited inside the app.

6) PERMISSIONS DISCLOSURE  (Xiaomi asks why each permission is needed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERNET              Fetch Quran text, tafsir, recitations and prayer times.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Prayer times by approximate city (user-initiated).
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION   Prayer times and Qibla direction (user-initiated).
RECORD_AUDIO           Optional voice search — only when the user starts it.
Optional feature: android.software.leanback (Android TV support, not required).

Note for the reviewer: location is used locally to compute prayer times and Qibla,
and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.
